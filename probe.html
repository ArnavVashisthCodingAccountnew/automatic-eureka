Which connection should I use?

Laptop: Chrome or Edge, USB cable, press USB cable.
Android phone: Chrome 148 or newer, USB-OTG cable, press USB cable. If that is missing, use Bluetooth with a BLE module (HM-10, ESP32, Nano 33 BLE). HC-05 and HC-06 do not work.
iPhone: Safari has neither. Plug the board into the laptop, press Host relay, then on the phone press Join and type the code. Bluetooth works only inside the Bluefy browser.
The relay needs the laptop and phone online. Venue Wi-Fi that isolates devices can block it; a phone hotspot usually works. Replay always works.
